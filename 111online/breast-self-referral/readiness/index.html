Preparing your organisation for onboarding

This page outlines the key requirements and activities involved in introducing NHS 111 online self-referral within your organisation. Our onboarding readiness checklist provides an overview of the operational, clinical, technical and governance arrangements that will need to be considered as part of the onboarding process. The NHS 111 online team will work with participating organisations throughout onboarding to support local planning, implementation and go-live preparations.

You do not need to do this alone. The NHS 111 online team will support you through every stage.

Who is this information for?

This guidance is intended for NHS Trusts, Cancer Alliances and clinical and operational leads involved in exploring or supporting the introduction of self-referral services.

What does onboarding involve?

The onboarding process typically includes:

  • Identifying key clinical and operational leads.
  • Understanding existing referral pathways and local processes.
  • Agreeing how self-referrals will be received, managed and processed.
  • Establishing clinical governance, safety and operational arrangements.
  • Agreeing data collection, reporting and communication requirements.
  • Completing pathway testing and confirming readiness for go-live.
